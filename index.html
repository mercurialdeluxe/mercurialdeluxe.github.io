02 — A note from uranium

Mercurial
Deluxe

Mercurial Deluxe is an independent, fan-made live version of Mercurial, the original album by chandol. I’m uranium, and I put this edition together from recordings of chandol performing the album on its corresponding tour.

I think it’s a way to experience the songs as they happened live, also offering an opportunity for fans who couldn't go. I started making this when the tour began, and am finally finished as of October 18th.

uranium — COMPOSER OF MERCURIAL DELUXE
